Cancellation and Refund Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This Cancellation and Refund Policy applies to all purchases, digital subscriptions, and service top-ups made on TSIS Tools (tools.thesouthindianschool.in), operated by The South Indian School.

1. Subscription Cancellation

  • Self-Service Cancellation: You can cancel your monthly subscription (₹199 / month) at any time directly through your account dashboard on the AI Study Suite page or by contacting our customer support team.
  • Effect of Cancellation: When you cancel a subscription, auto-renewal is terminated immediately. You will not be billed for subsequent billing cycles. Any unused AI requests credited for the active 30-day billing cycle remain valid and accessible until the end of that cycle.

2. Refund Policy

Because TSIS Tools provides digital services and instantaneous AI compute quota:

  • Failed AI Requests: If an AI request fails due to an internal server error or network issue on our infrastructure, the request quota is automatically restored to your account balance without requiring manual intervention.
  • Payment Technical Failures / Double Debits: If your bank account, card, UPI, or wallet is debited but the order fails or quota is not credited within 2 hours, the amount will either be automatically reversed by your payment provider or refunded by us upon verification.
  • General Policy: Payments for completed digital top-ups or active subscriptions that have already been activated and utilized are non-refundable, except in cases of verified billing errors, duplicate transactions, or where required by Indian consumer protection laws.

3. Refund Processing Timeline

Processing Window: Once a refund request is reviewed and approved, it will be initiated via our payment gateway (Razorpay) within 24 to 48 business hours.

Credit to Account: The refunded amount typically reflects in the original source account (UPI, credit/debit card, net banking, or wallet including Amazon Pay / PhonePe) within 5 to 7 business days, depending on your bank's settlement cycle.

4. How to Request a Refund

To request a refund or raise a billing grievance, please reach out with your registered email address and the Razorpay Payment ID:

  • Email: contact@thesouthindianschool.in
  • School Portal: thesouthindianschool.in
  • Contact Page: Contact Us
