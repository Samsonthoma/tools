Shipping and Exchange Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This Shipping and Exchange Policy applies to all digital services, learning applications, and top-up plans on TSIS Tools (tools.thesouthindianschool.in), operated by The South Indian School.

1. Nature of Products & Digital Delivery

100% Digital Services: TSIS Tools provides online educational software, interactive learning utilities, and AI-powered study assistance. We do not sell or ship any physical goods or tangible merchandise.

Physical Shipping: Because all products and services are completely digital and delivered online through web browsers, no physical shipping or courier dispatch is required or applicable.

2. Delivery Timeline & Fulfilment

  • Instant Delivery: Upon successful authorization and confirmation of payment via our payment gateway (Razorpay, UPI, Cards, Netbanking, or Wallets such as Amazon Pay and PhonePe), digital AI quota and subscription features are activated and credited to your account immediately (instantaneous electronic fulfilment).
  • Confirmation: An automated electronic payment confirmation receipt is generated by Razorpay and delivered to your registered email address upon checkout completion.
  • Access: All features are accessible immediately by signing into your Google account at https://tools.thesouthindianschool.in/ai-study-suite.html.

3. Product Exchange Policy

  • Because all items sold are digital compute requests and subscription entitlements activated directly into user accounts, physical exchange is not applicable.
  • If you inadvertently purchased the wrong plan or experienced any issue during activation, please notify us within 24 hours at contact@thesouthindianschool.in. Our team will review the issue and assist in applying the appropriate credit or resolution.
