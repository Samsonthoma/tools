Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy applies to TSIS Tools (tools.thesouthindianschool.in), operated by The South Indian School and developed by Samson Thomas.

Information we collect

  • Google sign-in: when you sign in, Google gives us your Google account ID, name, email address and profile picture. We use these only to identify your account, enforce usage quotas and record purchases. We never receive your Google password.
  • AI requests: text, images or audio you submit to AI features are sent through our server to Google Gemini to generate a response. Do not submit sensitive personal information.
  • Usage records: number of AI requests, request balances and payment references, stored in our database (Cloudflare D1).
  • Payments: processed by Razorpay. We do not see or store your card, UPI or bank details; we only receive payment and subscription IDs and status.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics collects anonymous usage statistics.

How we use it

To provide the tools, authenticate you, enforce quotas, process payments, prevent abuse and improve the site. We do not sell your data.

Service providers

Google (sign-in, Gemini, Analytics), Razorpay (payments) and Cloudflare (hosting, database). Their own privacy policies apply to data they handle.

Storage and retention

Your sign-in token is kept only in your browser session and cleared when you sign out or close the tab. Account and payment records are kept as long as needed for your account and legal/accounting obligations.

Children

Students under 18 should use the AI features and sign in only with permission of a parent or teacher.

Your rights

You may request access to or deletion of your account data by contacting us via the school website or the developer.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest revision.
